Beginner Safety Checklist: Avoid Costly Transfer Mistakes

Before clicking Withdraw, verify these three rules to protect your funds:

1. Match Networks 100%

Your withdrawal network (e.g., Arbitrum, Base, TRC20, or SOL) must match the deposit network selected in your receiving wallet or exchange. Sending to a mismatched network can result in permanent loss.

2. Send a Small Test First

For first-time transfers or large balances, send a minimum test amount (e.g., 5–10 USDT) first and confirm arrival before transferring the remaining funds.

3. Avoid High-Gas ERC20

Ethereum L1 (ERC20) withdrawal fees are dynamic and frequently cost 5–15+ USDT. Whenever your destination supports Layer 2s (Arbitrum, Optimism, Base, Polygon) or SOL/BSC, you can save over 90% on fees.
